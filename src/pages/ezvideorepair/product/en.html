A few useful answers

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Your Mac processes your videos and photos locally. Initial setup may need an internet connection to download files.

Does it work on Intel Macs or Windows?

Restoration currently supports Apple Silicon Macs only. Intel Macs and Windows are not supported.

How long does restoration take?

It depends on your Mac, the media and its duration. Processing may take much longer than playback. Start with a two-second preview to decide how much to process.

Do I need the original files when moving a project?

Yes. A project keeps your settings and restored results, and refers back to the original media. Copy both the project and the originals to your new Mac.

Where can I download the app?

Download for Mac (DMG) Open the DMG and drag EZVideoRepair into Applications.
