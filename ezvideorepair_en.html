Less distraction, more focus

The tools you need

A clear path from import to preview to export, without turning a simple restoration task into a complicated workspace.

01

Preview before you commit

Preview two seconds from any point. When EZVR Flash V1 restores a 1080p preview in tiles, it preserves the timing of the picture and soundtrack, even when the audio starts first.

02

Choose how much to process

Restore a full video or set a custom duration. Work on just the part you need.

03

Videos and images

Preview a section of a landscape or portrait video, or open a single photo to inspect the restored details.

04

Reopen a saved project

Reopen a saved project to view a finished video or continue from saved frames that match the current restoration settings.

05

Reuse finished results

Matching media and settings reuse completed results, reducing unnecessary processing.

06

Downloads stay in view

Setup files download in the background. Check their progress, pause the download, or resume it later.

From footage to results

Three steps to local restoration

  1. 01

    Install the app

    Open the DMG and drag EZVideoRepair into Applications. Launch the app and follow the setup prompts.

  2. 02

    Import and preview

    Import your media, choose a start time, and inspect a two-second preview.

  3. 03

    Process and save

    Choose a full video or a custom duration, export the result, or save a project to continue later.
