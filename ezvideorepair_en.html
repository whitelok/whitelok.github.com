A few useful answers

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Your Mac processes your videos and photos locally. Initial setup may need an internet connection to download files.

Does it work on Intel Macs or Windows?

Restoration currently supports Apple Silicon Macs only. Intel Macs and Windows are not supported.

How long does restoration take?

It depends on your Mac, the media and its duration. Processing may take much longer than playback. Start with a two-second preview to decide how much to process.

Do I need the original files when moving a project?

Yes. A project keeps your settings and restored results, and refers back to the original media. Copy both the project and the originals to your new Mac.

How much memory can memory-saving mode save?

Both INT8 and BF16 variants of V1 and V2 can load weights from SSD one layer at a time. V1 needs a compatible inference profile. In one test on an M4 Pro Mac with 24 GB of memory, restoring a 30-second video with V2 INT8 used 8.96 GB at peak, down from 12.13 GB. Processing took 379 seconds instead of 366. The mode is off by default.

How are different frame sizes handled?

V1 removes the padding it adds and keeps any black bars in the original. With the 512×512 square profile, it can restore larger photos and videos in overlapping tiles. V2 uses tiles for videos that exceed its whole-frame limit. Tiled frames can be up to 4,096 pixels per side and 9,437,184 pixels in total.
